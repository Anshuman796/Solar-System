Meet The Planets

Eight planets orbit our Sun, each with its own unique characteristics.

01

Mercury

TERRESTRIAL PLANET

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun. Mercury has extreme temperature differences between day and night.

☀ 57.9M km 📅 88 days
02

Venus

TERRESTRIAL PLANET

Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System, with a thick carbon dioxide atmosphere.

☀ 108.2M km 📅 225 days
03

Earth

TERRESTRIAL PLANET

Our home planet and the only world currently known to support life.

🌊 71% Water 📅 365.25 days
04

Mars

TERRESTRIAL PLANET

Known as the Red Planet because iron minerals in its soil give the surface its reddish color.

☀ 227.9M km 📅 687 days
05

Jupiter

GAS GIANT

Jupiter is the largest planet and is famous for its enormous storms, including the Great Red Spot.

☀ 778.5M km 📅 11.86 years
06

Saturn

GAS GIANT

Saturn is famous for its spectacular ring system made primarily of ice and rocky particles.

💍 Rings 📅 29.45 years
07

Uranus

ICE GIANT

Uranus rotates on its side, giving it an unusual axial tilt of about 98 degrees.

🧊 Ice Giant 📅 84 years
08

Neptune

ICE GIANT

Neptune is the farthest recognized planet from the Sun and has extremely fast winds.

🌬 2,000 km/h 📅 164.8 years
